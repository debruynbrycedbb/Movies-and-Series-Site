CINEVAULT — POSTER FILENAMES
==============================================================================

Save each poster into the images/ folder using the filename on the
left. .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work — just keep the name.

Series filenames carry a tv-- prefix so they can never clash with a
film of the same name.

Any title you skip falls back to the automatic lookup, so you can do
these a few at a time.

Added something since this list was made? Open it on the site and expand
"Poster wrong or missing? Fix it" — it shows you the filename.
